## 🌍 The Problem: Fragility of Modern Knowledge

In peacetime with stable electricity and high-speed cellular networks, humanity has access to all knowledge online. However, modern society is dangerously dependent on centralized cloud infrastructure:
- **During war, natural disasters, or EMP events**, cell towers go dark within hours due to backup generator fuel depletion or physical sabotage.
- **Critical survival and medical knowledge** is scattered across paywalled papers, unverified blogs, or streaming videos that cannot be loaded without internet access.
- **Mainstream applications leak metadata and telemetry**, creating serious risks for civilians and refugees during device checks at checkpoints.

**KOVCHEG (ARK)** was engineered from the ground up to solve this existential problem. It turns any ordinary smartphone, tablet, or laptop into an indestructible, autonomous survival terminal that functions **100% offline with zero cloud dependencies**.

## 🛡️ Core Philosophies

### 1. 100% Local Self-Sufficiency (Zero-Cloud / Zero-CDN)
Every single byte needed by KOVCHEG — fonts, icons, interactive calculators, search algorithms, audio synthesizers, maps, and 11 full field compendiums (~88 MB) — is stored directly on your physical hardware storage. It never attempts to reach an external server.

### 2. Evidence-Based Field Protocols
KOVCHEG rejects unverified survival myths. All medical, nuclear, and chemical defense guides adhere to verified international standards:
- **Trauma & Bleeding**: TCCC MARCH (Tactical Combat Casualty Care) protocols.
- **Dehydration & Epidemics**: World Health Organization (WHO) oral rehydration formulas.
- **Radiation & CBRN**: International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) and civil defense nuclear fallout decay physics (the 7/10 rule and halving thicknesses).
- **Mine & UXO Safety**: International Mine Action Standards (IMAS) Zero Touch doctrine.

### 3. Absolute Operational Security (OPSEC)
- **Zero Telemetry**: No trackers, no cookies, no analytics.
- **Master App Lock**: Full-screen gate preventing unauthorized physical inspection of the app.
- **Duress Code (Panic Wipe)**: Entering a decoy passcode unlocks benign wilderness cards while silently wiping private notes, cycle logs, and GPS caches.
- **Covert Hardware SOS**: 3 clicks on the physical power button triggers an emergency beacon with GPS coordinates.
